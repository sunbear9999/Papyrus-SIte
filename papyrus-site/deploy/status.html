Known limitations

  • No validated performance, retrieval-quality, extraction-accuracy, or model-accuracy benchmarks are published here.
  • No comparative environmental measurements are available. Local processing does not remove electricity use or upstream training impacts.
  • Non-generative parsing and classification can still misread, omit, or incorrectly connect material.
  • Source-evaluation signals are heuristic, not authoritative credibility judgments.
  • Prompt and context traces show recorded inputs and outputs, not hidden model reasoning.

Future possibilities

Possible future work includes broader testing, clearer extension isolation, accessibility evaluation, stronger status evidence, and eventual source sharing. These are directions rather than promises, dates, or a release roadmap. Development follows personal interest and available time.

Papyrus is a personal software and learning project. It is not currently available for download.

The site describes an experimental prototype; individual capabilities may be incomplete or change.
